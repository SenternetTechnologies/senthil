THE PRODUCT VISION

Two essentials.
One connected experience.

We’re starting with the tools people use every day, focusing on a coherent experience rather than feature overload.

⌘ In development
S

Senternet Browser

A focused Windows-first browsing experience with familiar navigation, useful controls, and security updates built into the plan.

  • Tabs, bookmarks and downloads
  • Clear privacy and permission controls
  • Integrated access to Senternet Mail
✉ In development
✉

Senternet Mail

A clean email experience designed for real communication, with account security, spam protection and dependable delivery as priorities.

  • Inbox, sent, drafts and search
  • Secure account and recovery flows
  • Reliable email delivery infrastructure

Product previews illustrate the planned direction. They are not representations of a live browser or operational email service.
